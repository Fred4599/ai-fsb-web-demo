# Pricing structure

## TIER 1 — Foundation

**Best for:** Contractors running their business from a cell phone, skeptical buyers, low point of entry, low risk, high reward.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Setup fee** | $97 |
| **Monthly** | $97/mo |

### Includes

- Basic Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system to replace their spreadsheets
- 1-page website
- Lead capture from website into CRM
- Basic lead follow-up sequence (email, 5 touches)
- Online booking calendar + **appointment reminders via email**
- Google Business Profile review and optimization
- Monthly performance report

### Notes

- **AI receptionist** (answers calls 24/7, qualifies leads, books appointments) is **not** included in Foundation. It will be offered **à la carte** later and is included in the **Dominator** plan.

---

## TIER 2 — Growth

**Best for:** Established contractors ready to scale/grow; Foundation customers looking for more automation.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Setup fee** | $297 |
| **Monthly** | $297/mo |

### Everything in Foundation, plus

- Missed-call **multi-channel follow-up** (text/SMS, email, voicemail) — SMS capability aligned with follow-up and messaging at this tier
- 5-page website with AI chat widget
- Automated customer review system with reputation management
- Quote/estimate follow-up sequence
- Reactivation and upsell campaigns (past customers, referral offers, service packages)
- Local SEO optimization + AI search optimization
- **Bi-weekly** performance review

### Tier distinction (reminders & SMS)

- **Foundation:** Appointment reminders via **email** only.
- **Growth:** Help setting up **SMS** for follow-up and multi-channel communication (e.g. missed-call and nurture flows), beyond email-only reminders.

---

## TIER 3 — Dominator

**Best for:** Growth-minded contractors who want to own their market.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Setup fee** | $997 |
| **Monthly** | $997/mo |

### Everything in Foundation & Growth, plus

- **AI receptionist** — answers calls 24/7, qualifies leads, books appointments
- **Social media** — automated creation and auto-posting
- Full AI-powered local SEO management
- Seasonal & promo broadcast campaigns with pre-built no-brainer templates
- Review display widget on website, linked to auto-publish on review websites
- Client-facing live dashboard for daily reporting
- Priority support + weekly strategy check-in call
- Lead generation funnels linked to Meta, Google, X, Reddit, Yahoo, Yelp
